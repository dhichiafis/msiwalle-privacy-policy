Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Msivu Wallet ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Msivu Wallet mobile application and related services.

Depending on the features you use, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

1.2 Financial and Transaction Information

  • Wallet balances and transaction records.
  • Deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and payment records supported by the application.

  • Financial goals, savings targets, and contributions you create using the application.

We process financial information to provide wallet features, maintain transaction histories, display balances, and support the financial management functions available in the app.

Our servers and infrastructure providers may process technical information needed to operate and secure the service. Depending on the configuration, this may include IP addresses, request timestamps, error logs, application version information, and basic diagnostic information.

1.4 Information You Provide

We may process information you submit through registration, profile creation, wallet operations, support requests, and other application features.

We use information, as applicable, to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Create wallets and display wallet balances.
  • Record and display transactions and transaction histories.
  • Process supported deposits, withdrawals, and transfers.
  • Support savings goals and financial tracking features.
  • Detect errors, investigate suspicious activity, and maintain application security.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service information.
  • Maintain accurate records and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Improve reliability, performance, and the user experience.

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the service unless we have an appropriate legal basis and, where required, have informed you and obtained your consent.

Msivu Wallet may store and process wallet balances, transaction details, and related financial records to provide its features. You should keep your login credentials and any transaction authentication details confidential.
